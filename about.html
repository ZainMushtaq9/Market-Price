About Market-Price

Market-Price is Pakistan's first comprehensive, multilingual agricultural market price platform. We provide real-time, accurate pricing information for fruits, vegetables, grains, and other commodities across major cities and mandis throughout Pakistan.

Our Mission

To democratize access to market price information for everyone in Pakistan - from farmers and traders to consumers and policymakers. We believe that transparent pricing leads to fairer markets and better economic outcomes for all stakeholders.

What We Do

We automatically collect and aggregate market prices from verified sources across Pakistan. Our platform updates multiple times daily to ensure you always have access to the most current information. Whether you're checking potato prices in Multan or comparing rice rates between Lahore and Karachi, we make it simple and accessible.

Who We Serve

  • Farmers: Get fair prices for your produce
  • Traders: Make informed buying decisions
  • Consumers: Find the best market rates
  • Researchers: Access market data and trends

Why Choose Us

Multilingual: We support English, Urdu, Roman Urdu, Punjabi, and Saraiki so everyone can access information in their preferred language.

User-Friendly: No complicated interfaces. Just ask your question and get instant answers.

Always Free: We believe market information should be accessible to all, regardless of economic status.
